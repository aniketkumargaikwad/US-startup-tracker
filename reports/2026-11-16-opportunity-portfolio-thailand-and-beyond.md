# Opportunity Portfolio: Thailand Tourism and a Wider Scan

**Research date:** 16 November 2026 · Third report in this series, after the [US startup analysis](2026-11-15-us-startup-opportunity-analysis.md) and the [India addendum](2026-11-15-india-adaptation-addendum.md).

**Founder profile used for scoring:** a software engineer in Maharashtra, working largely alone, building most of the product with Claude, open to any country.

Evidence labels are unchanged: **[P]** primary, **[M]** major outlet, **[C]** company claim, **[E]** third-party estimate, **[U]** unverified or single weak source, **[A]** my assumption. I worked from search-result summaries and could not open source pages directly, so treat each link as the place to verify a claim.

## What this report concludes

1. **Your Thailand observation is correct and backed by data, but selling websites or apps to Thai operators is the wrong shape.** A small operator with a new website still has no customers. The shape that can work starts from the demand side: a chat concierge for Indian travellers in Thailand that brings bookings to Thai operators, who then get a free tool that handles the language for them.
2. **Across everything screened, five opportunities pass an evidence test.** Two are strong on evidence (money recovery in India; a tender-bid assistant for Indian small businesses). Thailand travel is promising in the demand-first shape. Two more are real but have a specific obstacle (EU carbon reporting for exporters; app-store products).
3. **Desk research can show that a need is real, already paid for, and under-served. It cannot show that you can win customers.** Each opportunity below therefore ends with a test of two to four weeks that can prove it wrong cheaply.

### Why "build them a website or app" does not work

1. **The missing piece is customers, not a website.** Tourists find things on maps, booking platforms and social media. A new site gets no visitors on its own.
2. **Thai small businesses pay very little for software.** Chat tools sell for about ฿500–3,000 a month, roughly $15–85 [E].
3. **Selling needs Thai language and presence.** Foreign software companies entering Thailand have gone through local partners and agencies [E].
4. **Foreigners may not run tour businesses.** Secondary legal sources say tour operation is closed to foreigners and travel agencies need at least 51% Thai ownership; 40 licences were revoked in 2024 over ownership rules [E]. A foreign company can sell software, but taking payment for tours needs a licensed Thai partner. This needs a Thai lawyer's confirmation.
5. **Hotels are already served.** Botnoi, HiJiffy, Yanolja, OKKAMI, Bookola and others sell multilingual guest messaging on LINE and WhatsApp [E].
6. **The market is soft.** Arrivals are down about 4.6% this year.

Sources: [Chat tool pricing](https://botsphere.chat/) · [Zwiz pricing](https://zwiz.ai/en) · [Foreign entrant using a local agency](https://blog.cresclab.com/th/crescendo-lab-targeting-the-thailand-market) · [Ownership rules](https://www.ilct.co.th/?p=16506) · [Licences revoked](https://aseannow.com/topic/1354910-thailand-cracks-down-on-foreign-tour-operators-40-licences-revoked/) · [Botnoi](https://botnoigroup.com/ai/chatbot/overview) · [HiJiffy on LINE](https://hijiffy.com/?p=3066) · [Yanolja AI concierge](https://www.thailand-business-news.com/pr-news/yanolja-cloud-solution-introduces-ai-concierge-for-hotel-operations) · [OKKAMI on LINE](https://okkami.com/?p=13468)

### The shape that can work: bring the customers first

**Product:** a WhatsApp concierge for Indian travellers in Thailand, in Hindi, Marathi and English. It answers questions, recommends and books tours, activities and transfers, finds vegetarian and Jain food, and takes payment in rupees. Behind it, software messages the Thai operator in Thai and confirms the booking. Operators get this as a free tool inside LINE because you are bringing them paying customers.

**Why Indian travellers**

- 2.48 million Indians visited Thailand in 2025, the third-largest source market; the tourism authority targets 2.55 million in 2026 [M].
- You can reach them in your own languages and through Indian travel creators. You cannot reach Thai operators that way.
- Thailand's tourism authority is itself urging operators to add Hindi information, vegetarian options and digital payments [E]. One market report estimates 39% of Indian visitors are fully vegetarian, with no stated method [U].
- From April 2026 the tax collected at source on overseas tour packages fell to a flat 2%, from 5% and 20% [M].

**Where the money comes from:** a Thai destination-management company publishes agent rates 25–30% below retail, and 30–35% for volume [E]. That margin pays you without charging operators anything.

**Proof the model exists:** AutoReserve in Japan lets foreigners book restaurants through an app while AI phones the restaurant in Japanese. It reports 3.1 million registered users and over 10,000 reservations a month, and restaurants pay nothing [C]. Note the modest ratio of bookings to users.

**Competition:** Thrillophilia, Klook, Headout and MakeMyTrip sell packaged activities to Indians. Large Maharashtra tour companies sell group tours. None is built as an in-trip chat service covering the long tail of small operators [A]. Half of travellers already use AI for recommendations at their destination, but only about 11% have booked through AI [E], so trust in the booking step is the thing to earn.

**Test (four weeks, and the timing is good: Indian peak travel runs December to February)**

1. Pick one destination Indians favour, such as Phuket or Pattaya.
2. Sign one licensed Thai partner for 30–50 experiences at agent rates. Confirm the legal structure with a Thai lawyer and an Indian chartered accountant.
3. Run the concierge by hand on WhatsApp with Claude drafting replies and Thai messages.
4. Reach travellers through two or three small Indian travel creators and trip-planning groups.

Continue if 30 paid bookings arrive in four weeks, the margin after payment costs is at least 15%, and fewer than one booking in ten goes wrong. Stop if travellers ask questions but book elsewhere.

**Risks:** something going wrong for a customer abroad; platforms adding the same chat service; seasonality; foreign-exchange and tax compliance on the Indian side; dependence on one Thai partner.

## Part 2: The wider scan

### 1. Money-recovery assistant (India) — score 75

The health-insurance idea in the India addendum is one case of a larger pattern: money that belongs to people is stuck behind paperwork, and small companies already charge to get it out.

| Pool of stuck money | Official or reported size | Who charges for recovery today |
|---|---|---|
| Rejected health insurance claims | About 26 lakh repudiated claims a year; ₹26,037 crore rejected in 2023-24 (see addendum) | Insurance Samadhan |
| Provident fund claims | About one in five claims rejected in 2024-25, down from 29% in 2021-22 [M] | Kustodian: upfront fee plus a success-linked fee; says it has recovered over ₹150 crore [C] |
| Unclaimed shares held by the investor protection fund | ₹89,004 crore across 1,671 companies as of November 2025; another estimate says about ₹1 lakh crore [M] | Share Samadhan, a listed company |
| Unclaimed mutual fund dividends and redemptions | ₹3,811 crore at March 2026 [M] | — |

**Two cautions.** Share Samadhan's revenue roughly halved in 2025-26 and profit fell about 81% [E], which shows this work is lumpy when done by people. And the provident fund body announced faster, more automated settlement in June 2026, which may shrink that pool.

**Why it fits:** each case is reading documents and drafting a structured claim, which Claude does well. The rules are Indian. Fees can be tied to money recovered.

Scores: demand 16, monetization 13, market 13, pain 14, competitive opportunity 6, feasibility 8, global 5.

**Test:** as in the addendum. A lawyer's opinion first, then 20 real cases handled by hand, charging from the sixth.

### 2. Tender-bid assistant for small businesses (India) — score 72

**US model:** GovDash, which helps companies win government contracts, raised a $30M Series B in January 2026 and says customers won over $5B in contracts on it in 2025 [P][C]. The same idea is funded in Europe: AutogenAI ($22.3M), Altura (€8M), BidScript [E].

**The Indian market**

- The government marketplace passed ₹5 lakh crore of orders in 2025-26 [M].
- It has nearly 25 lakh sellers; about 72% of active sellers are micro and small enterprises [M].
- Over 11 lakh small enterprises received orders worth ₹2.36 lakh crore in the year [M].

**Who serves it**

| Player | Focus | Price or funding |
|---|---|---|
| ContraVault AI | Large infrastructure firms; says 40 clients including Tata and Adani; now expanding to the US [C] | $3.1M round, June 2026 [E] |
| TendersAgent, BidIndia | Tender alerts and documents | ₹29,999 and ₹24,999 a year [E] |
| BidShakti | AI bid preparation | About $25 a month [E] |
| BidAssist and others | Tender search | Free app, pay per tender [E] |

**The gap:** the funded player sells to large firms and is heading abroad. Small sellers get alert services, not help preparing a bid that survives evaluation. Vendor blogs name incomplete documents, expired certificates and price-entry mistakes as the common reasons for rejection [E]. I found no official rejection statistics, which is the weak point in this case.

**Product:** upload the tender; get a checklist of eligibility conditions matched against your certificates, warnings on anything expired or missing, a drafted technical bid, and a price check. Sold per bid or by annual subscription.

Scores: demand 15, monetization 14, market 13, pain 11, competitive opportunity 5, feasibility 8, global 6.

**Test (three weeks):** interview 15 sellers who bid on the marketplace; Maharashtra's industrial belts are a natural place to find them [A]. Ask to see their last rejected bid. Prepare five live bids by hand for a fee. Continue if at least half have lost a bid on a technicality in the past year and three of five pay.

### 5. EU carbon-border reporting for exporters (India) — score 63

The EU's carbon border rules entered their binding phase on 1 January 2026. Exporters of steel, aluminium and four other goods must supply emissions data checked by an accredited verifier, or face penal default values [E]. Small firms often lack plant-level data [E]. One estimate puts India's covered exports to the EU above €8 billion a year [U].

CleanCarbon.ai already claims 200 to 500 exporters and a partnership with a government-backed export council [C]. The work needs emissions-accounting knowledge and a verifier relationship, which a solo software engineer does not have. The government has reportedly said it will cover certification costs for small exporters [U], which could help or could remove the buyer's urgency.

Worth pursuing only with a domain partner. Scores: demand 13, monetization 13, market 9, pain 13, competitive opportunity 5, feasibility 4, global 6.

Sources: [Rules enter binding phase](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/economy/eus-carbon-rules-enter-implementation-phase-putting-indian-msmes-under-pressure) · [Small steel firms](https://india.mongabay.com/2026/06/european-union-border-tax-tests-indias-small-iron-and-steel-businesses/) · [CleanCarbon.ai](https://www.alcircle.com/news/cleancarbon-ai-simplifying-cbam-compliance-for-indian-aluminium-and-steel-exporters-111492)

### 6. App-store products (Shopify) — a path, not yet an idea

Shopify says it paid developers more than $1 billion in 2025, and developers keep 100% of their first $1 million in app revenue [P]. About 80% of developers have a single app [E]. This suits one engineer selling worldwide in dollars with no sales calls.

I have not identified a specific app, so there is no score. The next step is to read low-star reviews of the top apps in two or three categories and look for a repeated complaint nobody has fixed. I can do that as a separate piece of research.

## Suggested order of work

One person cannot test five things at once. My suggested order:

1. **Now to February: the Thailand concierge test.** It is time-sensitive because Indian peak travel is starting, and it is the idea you came to yourself.
2. **In parallel, three weeks of tender interviews.** These are local conversations that need no product.
3. **Money recovery:** start with the lawyer's opinion, which takes a week and gates everything else.

Whichever of the three shows people paying first becomes the product. The others stop.
